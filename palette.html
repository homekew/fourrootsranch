Home · Brand palette

Warm, clean, modern, calm.

Seven colors with clear roles. White carries the page; paper warms the occasional band; olive grounds the dark moments and the wordmark; navy carries every important call to action; tan stays small.

Recommended balance

Use white as the dominant color. Keep tan as a tiny accent only.

White 70% Paper 15% Charcoal 7% Olive 5% Navy 2% Tan 1%
