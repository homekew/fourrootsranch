Four Roots Ranch is in a transition season, and the current priority is steady, high-quality care for the residents already at home on the ranch.

That means new intake is paused for now while the sanctuary refines its next program model. The animals currently in care are thriving, and this quieter stretch creates the space to strengthen daily systems, improve planning, and keep every routine centered on wellbeing.

Supporters can still help in meaningful ways:

- Follow the sanctuary story on Substack and Instagram
- Contribute to daily care through donations or needed goods
- Reach out about partnership opportunities that fit the current season

This site relaunch is part of that same effort to build a clearer, more flexible foundation for what comes next.
