Rehoming an animal can come with urgency, grief, and a long list of unknowns. This site rebuild preserves that support while making the path clearer for owners, adopters, and community members who want to help responsibly.

## What support looks like right now

Four Roots Ranch can help share rehoming needs through its network, offer guidance on preparing an animal for placement, and connect people with helpful resources. Because sanctuary capacity changes over time, intake cannot be guaranteed.

## If you need to rehome an animal

- Gather recent veterinary information, feeding notes, and housing details before reaching out.
- Include the animal's age, species, sex, temperament, and any known medical history.
- Be ready to describe current herd or flock dynamics, transportation options, and urgency.

## If you hope to adopt

Sanctuary residents are not listed for adoption, but the ranch may help connect potential adopters with owners who are trying to place animals safely. Joining the outreach list does not create an obligation to adopt.

## If your situation involves domestic pets or wildlife

Domestic pets are best referred to local humane organizations. Wildlife should be directed to licensed wildlife rehabilitation organizations such as Think Wild or the Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife.
