Four Roots Ranch offers donation-based group visits, service days, and partnership experiences for organizations that want to connect with the sanctuary in a respectful way.

## What to expect

- Visits are arranged for groups rather than drop-in public attendance
- Experiences may include a sanctuary introduction, resident stories, and guided discussion about care and rescue work
- Seasonal availability changes based on weather, staffing, and what is best for the animals

## Important notes

The sanctuary does not offer one-off public tours or regular open hours. Larger group visits may be paused during winter so daily animal routines can stay stable and comfortable.
