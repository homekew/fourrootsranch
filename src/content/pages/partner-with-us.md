Four Roots Ranch welcomes partnerships that are practical, mission-aligned, and genuinely helpful to animal care.

## Ways to get involved

- Sponsor a group work day or seasonal project
- Organize a supply drive for feed, bedding, or farm materials
- Host a fundraiser that directly benefits the sanctuary
- Arrange an intentional group visit tied to education or team building

Because the sanctuary is small and family run, partner events are planned selectively to protect the animals' routines and wellbeing.
