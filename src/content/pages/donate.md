Donations keep the sanctuary moving through routine and emergency care alike. They support hay and feed, minerals, bedding, fencing repairs, shelter materials, veterinary appointments, transportation, annual shearing, and hoof trimming.

## Monetary giving

Flexible donations help the ranch respond where support is needed most, from seasonal feed costs to unexpected medical needs. Gifts are tax-deductible, and the sanctuary EIN is listed on this page for donor records.

## Donations of goods

Useful year-round donations include:

- Bagged feed, supplements, and fresh produce
- Pine shavings, straw, and cold-weather bedding
- Tarps, shelters, and basic farm building materials
- Fence panels, gates, troughs, and buckets
- First-aid supplies, vitamins, and deworming support
- Gift cards for farm, hardware, and bulk-supply stores

## Welcome Home Fund

The sanctuary also runs a targeted giving path for intake-related onboarding costs, including exams, screenings, and transition supplies for new residents.
