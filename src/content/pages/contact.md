The sanctuary is family run, so the best way to reach out is through a clear written message with the details needed to route the request well.

## Good reasons to use this form

- Partnership or sponsorship interest
- Press and storytelling inquiries
- Questions about sanctuary programs or educational support
- Follow-up on donations, goods, or community events

For urgent animal placement questions, the rehome page is the better starting point because it gathers the information needed to review those situations responsibly.
