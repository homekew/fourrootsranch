A forever home where second chances grow old.

A 501(c)(3) family-run sanctuary in Bend, Oregon

We provide lifelong care for more than 100 alpacas, llamas, and other farm animals. Many of them seniors. All of them family.

Support our herd Read our story

We're a family of six in Bend, Oregon, and Four Roots Ranch is named for our four children. Our four roots.

Our journey with animals began in 2017, when we connected with a rescue rehoming alpacas in need. We took in twenty, thinking we were simply giving them a soft place to settle. They became part of our family almost immediately, and over time, the work grew from there.

In 2019, Four Roots Ranch became a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Today, more than 100 animals call our sanctuary home, and we are the largest sanctuary in the United States dedicated to alpacas and llamas.

The animals who come to us are often older, disabled, overlooked, or in need of a slower, safer life. Some have come from difficult situations. Some have come from families who loved them but could no longer keep them. However they arrive, our goal is the same: steady care, dignity, and a home for the rest of their lives.

— Kate, founder

A safe haven, especially for seniors.

Our sanctuary provides a safe haven for animals that have endured abuse, abandonment, and neglect, and for those whose owners can no longer care for them due to significant life changes. We are also home to several disabled animals and a large population of seniors who require special attention.

Nine species. One herd.

While alpacas and llamas are our specialty, we also care for sheep, goats, pigs, chickens, ducks, turkeys, and cats. We share their stories quietly and honestly, to raise awareness about compassionate care, the unique needs of each animal, and the kind of ownership that protects them.

Nala is one of our oldest residents. She came to us in our second year and has been part of every shearing weekend since. At 23, she's still thriving, a quiet reminder that consistent care, year after year, is the heart of what we do.
